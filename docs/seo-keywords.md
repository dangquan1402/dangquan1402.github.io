## Notes & Strategy
- **Platform Integrity:** All keywords and metadata target iOS and iPhone exclusively. No macOS keywords are used.
- **Privacy Angle:** "No watermark", "offline", and "no upload" represent strong user intent for privacy-conscious users handling tax, lease, and medical paperwork.
- **Single Primary Keyword Rule:** Every page focuses on one primary job in its Title and H1, followed by secondary benefits in the body text.
