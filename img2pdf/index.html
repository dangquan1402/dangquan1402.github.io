Convert Photos to PDF on iPhone – Free, Private, No Watermark

img2pdf is a free, on-device iPhone app by Quan Dang that turns photos, receipts, paper documents, and scans into clean, compact PDFs. No watermark, no account registration, and zero cloud uploads.

Your documents are processed entirely inside your iPhone sandbox. Everything works offline, preserving your privacy whether you are scanning tax paperwork, medical forms, or business receipts.

How It Works in 3 Steps

  1. Select or Scan: Pick photos from your photo library in the order you want, take new photos with the camera, or scan documents using automatic edge detection.
  2. Organize & Clean: Drag and drop pages to rearrange, crop with perspective correction, rotate, adjust brightness/contrast, or add a signature.
  3. Choose Size & Share: Select your desired page size (US Letter, A4, or original) and compression preset with a live size estimate, then share or save to Files.

Key Features

  • Flexible page capture: Scan paper with automatic edge detection, snap photos, import images from your camera roll, or bring in existing PDFs from the Files app.
  • Visual page ordering: Hold and drag pages to reorder them visually, sort by capture date, or reverse page sequence in one tap.
  • Page layouts & sizes: Export to standard US Letter, A4, Legal, or original image dimensions. Fit 1 to 4 images per sheet in a clean grid.
  • Image enhancements: Perspective crop, rotation, brightness and contrast tuning, and automatic document filters that remove shadows and clarify text.
  • Searchable text (OCR): On-device text recognition embeds an invisible layer into the PDF so you can search and copy text from scanned pages.
  • Sign & annotate: Draw your signature in black, blue, or red, add initials, checkmarks, and text fields directly to contracts and forms.
  • Redaction: Black out sensitive personal or financial information; redactions are permanently burned into the PDF.
  • Compression presets: Small, Standard, and Best Quality presets show exact file size estimates before sharing so attachments fit in emails.
  • Password protection: Secure sensitive documents with standard 128-bit AES encryption upon export.
  • 100% private & offline: Works completely offline with no network connection required. No tracking, no analytics, and no accounts.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does img2pdf add a watermark to my PDF?

No. There is no watermark on any exported PDF. An optional text watermark is available in the export settings if you choose to turn it on, but it is disabled by default.

Do my photos get uploaded to a cloud server?

No. All conversion, cropping, text recognition, and signing happen locally on your iPhone. The app works 100% offline, requires no account, and collects zero data.

Can I make the exported PDF searchable?

Yes. img2pdf includes on-device OCR that detects text on your pages and writes it as an invisible text layer in the PDF, allowing you to search and select words.

Can I sign documents or fill out forms before exporting?

Yes. You can draw signatures, add dates, initials, checkmarks, and text boxes directly onto any page. All annotations are permanently flattened into the PDF.

How does img2pdf keep PDF file sizes small for email?

img2pdf offers Small, Standard, and Best Quality presets with an instant file size estimate before sharing, so you can email documents without exceeding attachment limits.
