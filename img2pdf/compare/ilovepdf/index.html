img2pdf vs iLovePDF

iLovePDF is a multi-platform PDF suite that handles dozens of server-based file transformations including Office format conversions, document splitting, and cloud archiving. img2pdf is an on-device native iOS tool dedicated to converting photos and camera scans into clean, small PDFs without uploading documents to remote cloud servers.

By Quan Dang · Published November 2026

Overview: Broad Multi-Tool Cloud Suite vs Focused Private Scanner

iLovePDF originated as a popular web service providing every conceivable PDF manipulation tool: merging, splitting, converting Word or Excel documents to PDF, removing passwords, and adding page stamps. Its mobile app brings these web utilities to iOS, but many operations rely on sending files over the internet to remote servers.

img2pdf approaches document creation with a different philosophy: doing one job exceptionally well on the device. It focuses on turning photos, paper scans, and receipts into standardized, searchable, and compact PDFs while keeping your private data strictly inside your iPhone hardware sandbox. Here is how they compare.

Where iLovePDF Is Better

iLovePDF offers extensive document conversion features that go far beyond standard photo scanning:

  • Broad file format support: It can convert Microsoft Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and HTML documents into PDFs, and extract PDF content back into editable Office formats.
  • Cross-device desktop access: Its web-first architecture means you can start a task on an iPhone and access the same document tools on Windows, Mac, or Linux through any web browser.
  • Advanced PDF utilities: It includes tools for rotating pages, extracting text, splitting large books into chapters, and adding complex watermarks across hundreds of pages at once.

Where img2pdf Is Better

When the task is turning photos and paper into clean PDFs, img2pdf provides a faster, safer, and cleaner iOS experience:

  • Complete data privacy: With iLovePDF, your tax forms, bank statements, and personal identity documents are uploaded to third-party web servers. With img2pdf, your files never leave your phone.
  • Works anywhere without internet: img2pdf operates with full functionality on planes, subways, or remote locations with zero cellular or Wi-Fi connectivity.
  • No paywalls, subscriptions, or ads: There are no monthly fees, annoying banner advertisements, or artificial daily conversion limits.
  • Superior photo and scan assembly: img2pdf features a visual canvas where you can drag and drop page thumbnails into order, sort by photo date, or format 1–4 photos onto each page in a clean grid.
  • Instant file size feedback: View accurate size estimates before exporting so you can be confident your email attachment will not bounce.

Verdict: When to Use Each

Choose iLovePDF if: You need to convert Microsoft Word or Excel documents to PDF, split complex PDF books, or need access to PDF tools from a desktop web browser.

Choose img2pdf if: You are capturing physical paperwork, receipts, or photos on your iPhone, value absolute privacy, need to work offline, and want a completely free app with no subscription fees or daily quotas.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does iLovePDF upload my documents to the cloud?

Yes. The majority of iLovePDF's conversion features upload your documents to remote cloud servers for processing. In contrast, img2pdf processes all images, scans, and text locally on your iPhone sandbox without transmitting any data over the internet.

Can img2pdf convert Word or Excel documents?

No. img2pdf is specifically built for photo-to-PDF conversion, camera document scanning, and receipt packaging. If you need to convert Word, Excel, or PowerPoint presentations to PDF, iLovePDF is better suited for that task.

Are there daily conversion limits in img2pdf?

No. While iLovePDF limits the number of free tasks you can perform per day without a premium subscription, img2pdf has no daily limits, allowing you to convert as many documents as you need for free.
