img2pdf vs Apple Notes Scanner

Apple Notes document scanner is pre-installed on every iPhone, making it ideal for quickly capturing physical receipts and filing them directly into personal note notebooks. img2pdf is a dedicated on-device document scanner and PDF compiler designed for multi-page document organization, custom page sizing, redaction, and direct email sharing.

By Quan Dang · Published November 2026

Overview: The Built-in Tool vs A Dedicated Utility

Every modern iPhone running iOS comes equipped with Apple's built-in document scanner located inside the Notes and Files applications. For simple everyday paperwork, it is remarkably capable and completely free. However, when users need to compile mixed documents, rearrange page sequences visually, standardize page layouts for international offices, or compress large attachments for email delivery, standalone utilities like img2pdf provide specialized control.

Where Apple Notes Scanner Is Better

Apple Notes is a world-class system application that excels in several areas:

  • Zero download required: Notes is already installed on your iPhone out of the box, requiring zero storage setup or App Store downloads.
  • Integrated notebook context: Scans sit directly inside notes alongside rich text headings, interactive checklists, audio recordings, tables, and Apple Pencil drawings.
  • Device accessibility: Notes backed by your Apple ID appear across your iPad and Mac with zero extra configuration.
  • Control Center shortcut: You can add a quick-scan tile to your iOS Control Center or Lock Screen to start scanning paper in a single tap.

Where img2pdf Is Better

img2pdf focuses entirely on the output quality and structural flexibility of the resulting PDF document:

  • Visual drag-and-drop page sorting: Rather than rescanning if a page is out of sequence, img2pdf provides a visual grid where you can drag and drop pages into order, reverse the order, or sort by the photo capture timestamp.
  • Multiple images per sheet & paper sizes: You can format pages into standardized international standards (A4 or US Letter) and place 1, 2, or 4 images on each page, which is ideal for front-and-back ID cards or multiple small receipts.
  • Accurate compression estimates: When preparing PDFs for email attachments, img2pdf lets you pick Small, Standard, or Best quality presets while viewing a live file size estimate before you share.
  • Permanent redactions: You can black out private identifiers like Social Security numbers, bank details, or home addresses; the redaction is burned directly into the PDF raster so it cannot be undone or recovered.
  • Password security: You can apply standard 128-bit AES encryption to lock confidential files with a password before sending.

Verdict: Which One Should You Use?

Choose Apple Notes Scanner if: You want to quickly digitize a single receipt or document and keep it stored in your personal digital filing system across your Apple devices with minimal friction.

Choose img2pdf if: You are compiling multi-page documents from existing camera photos or scans, need to reorder pages visually, require standard US Letter or A4 dimensions, need to redact sensitive data, or must ensure the final file size is small enough to clear strict email attachment limits.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does either app put watermarks on documents?

Neither tool adds unwanted promotional watermarks. Apple Notes generates clean PDF exports without logos, and img2pdf generates clean, unwatermarked documents by default (offering an optional custom text watermark only if you choose to enable it).

Do my scanned documents get uploaded to third-party servers?

No. Both tools operate locally on your device for document capture. img2pdf has no servers, no analytics, and does not require an internet connection, ensuring complete on-device privacy.

Can I search text inside documents scanned with both tools?

Yes. Apple Notes leverages iOS Live Text to locate words within notes. img2pdf includes an on-device OCR engine that writes detected text into an invisible PDF layer, making the exported PDF file searchable inside third-party PDF viewers on Mac, Windows, and mobile devices.

Which tool makes smaller PDF files for email?

img2pdf provides superior control over file size. While Apple Notes applies a fixed compression algorithm without user controls or size feedback, img2pdf gives you Small, Standard, and Best quality presets with a live size estimate before exporting.
