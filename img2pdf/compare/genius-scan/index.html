img2pdf vs Genius Scan

Genius Scan is an established document scanning application developed by The Grizzly Labs known for its broad multi-cloud integrations and enterprise archiving options. img2pdf is a streamlined, completely free on-device iPhone scanner focused on privacy, visual page reordering, and direct file size estimation.

By Quan Dang · Published November 2026

Overview: Mature Cloud Archiving vs Lightweight On-Device Simplicity

Genius Scan has been a mainstay on the iOS App Store for over a decade. Built by French development studio The Grizzly Labs, it is widely praised for respecting user privacy and performing image processing locally on device. However, access to key features such as optical character recognition (OCR), auto-upload to cloud destinations, and PDF encryption requires purchasing Genius Scan+ or subscribing to Genius Cloud.

img2pdf takes a focused, free approach: all capabilities—including on-device OCR, compression size estimation, password encryption, and redaction—are included without in-app purchases or subscription paywalls. Here is how the two compare in detail.

Where Genius Scan Is Better

Genius Scan is a mature document tool that delivers outstanding functionality for business power users:

  • Direct cloud auto-export: In the paid Genius Scan+ tier, you can configure automatic upload rules to send scans directly to corporate cloud services including Dropbox, Google Drive, OneDrive, Box, Nextcloud, and WebDAV servers.
  • Smart document tagging and naming: It offers customizable file-naming templates based on location, document category, and date tags to automate document filing.
  • Enterprise MDM support: Large organizations can deploy and manage Genius Scan through Mobile Device Management (MDM) systems with custom configuration profiles.
  • Long-standing track record: Developed continuously since the early days of iOS, it possesses refined camera perspective algorithms and edge detection.

Where img2pdf Is Better

img2pdf excels for users who want complete functionality without paying for upgrades or managing account tiers:

  • Everything is free with no paywalls: Searchable text recognition (OCR), password encryption, and file compression are completely free, whereas Genius Scan gates OCR and encryption behind Genius Scan+.
  • Numbered photo picker & drag-and-drop: Easily pick photos from your library with numbered badges showing selection order, and drag-and-drop thumbnails visually to rearrange pages before generating the PDF.
  • Live compression size estimation: Choose between Small, Standard, or Best quality presets and see an accurate resulting file size before sharing, preventing oversized email attachments.
  • Multiple images per sheet: Lay out 1, 2, or 4 images on each page in a clean grid, which is particularly useful for consolidating receipts or multi-sided identity cards.
  • Permanent local redaction: Black out confidential banking or personal numbers permanently before distributing files.

Verdict: When to Use Each

Choose Genius Scan if: You need automatic background syncing to specialized business cloud platforms like Box, WebDAV, or OneDrive, or require corporate MDM deployment across an enterprise fleet.

Choose img2pdf if: You want a completely free scanner with on-device OCR, password encryption, custom page layouts, and instant file size control without paying for a Plus upgrade.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do both apps process documents on-device?

Yes. Both Genius Scan and img2pdf process documents locally on your phone rather than sending image data to remote cloud servers by default, making both choices far more secure than browser upload tools.

Is OCR text recognition free in both apps?

No. Genius Scan requires an upgrade to Genius Scan+ or a Genius Cloud subscription to enable OCR text recognition. In img2pdf, on-device OCR is completely free and included by default.

Can I password-protect PDFs in these apps?

In Genius Scan, PDF password encryption is a premium feature locked behind Genius Scan+. In img2pdf, you can apply 128-bit AES password encryption to any PDF for free.
