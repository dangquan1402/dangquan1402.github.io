img2pdf vs Adobe Scan

Adobe Scan is a cloud-connected document scanner from Adobe that integrates with the broader Acrobat ecosystem and automatically syncs files to Adobe Document Cloud. img2pdf is a lightweight, 100% on-device iPhone scanner designed for users who want clean, compact PDFs with complete offline privacy and zero sign-ups.

By Quan Dang · Published November 2026

Overview: Ecosystem Integration vs Local Device Privacy

Adobe Scan is one of the most widely recognized scanner applications on the App Store, backed by Adobe's long-standing document processing software. It offers reliable boundary detection and automatic text recognition, but relies heavily on user accounts and cloud uploads to Adobe Document Cloud.

By contrast, img2pdf focuses strictly on fast, local document conversion where your paperwork, receipts, and sensitive documents never leave your iPhone sandbox. Here is a fair, factual comparison of the two approaches in 2026.

Where Adobe Scan Is Better

Adobe Scan is an established enterprise tool with genuine strengths for specific workflows:

  • Adobe Acrobat integration: Scans sync directly into your Adobe Document Cloud account, making them instantly accessible within Adobe Acrobat Reader on desktop and web.
  • Cross-platform availability: Adobe Scan runs on both iOS and Android, allowing cross-device access for multi-platform teams.
  • Business card recognition: Scans of business cards can automatically extract contact details and save them to your contacts.
  • Advanced cloud OCR: Server-side optical character recognition handles difficult typography and multi-language documents effectively.

Where img2pdf Is Better

img2pdf is built from the ground up for individual iPhone users who prioritize speed, privacy, and simplicity:

  • No account required: You can open the app and generate a PDF in seconds without creating an Adobe ID, providing an email, or signing into an account.
  • Strictly on-device privacy: Documents, receipts, and tax papers are processed entirely within the iPhone sandbox. No files are ever sent to external cloud servers.
  • True offline capability: img2pdf functions seamlessly with airplane mode enabled or in locations without cellular data or Wi-Fi reception.
  • Free file compression: While Adobe requires an Acrobat subscription to access file compression controls, img2pdf includes Small, Standard, and Best quality compression presets with an accurate live size estimate before exporting.
  • Multi-image sheet layouts: Put 1, 2, or 4 images on a single sheet of paper to create front-and-back ID card summaries or compact receipt packages.

Verdict: When to Use Each

Choose Adobe Scan if: You already pay for Adobe Creative Cloud or Acrobat Pro, need documents synchronized automatically across desktop workstations, or work in a corporate environment standardized around Adobe services.

Choose img2pdf if: You want an instant, free utility that requires no login, never uploads sensitive personal documents to third-party servers, works completely offline, and lets you compress PDFs easily for email attachments.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I have to create an account to use Adobe Scan or img2pdf?

Adobe Scan requires you to sign in with an Adobe ID, Google, or Apple account before you can use the scanner. In contrast, img2pdf has no account system, no sign-in screen, and collects zero personal user details.

Are my scans uploaded to cloud servers?

Adobe Scan uploads scanned files to Adobe Document Cloud servers to perform text recognition and provide cloud access. img2pdf performs all scanning, text recognition, and compression locally on your iPhone hardware, ensuring zero cloud exposure.

Can both apps produce searchable text (OCR)?

Yes. Adobe Scan uses cloud processing to generate searchable text, while img2pdf uses on-device Apple Vision machine learning to embed an invisible text layer locally without sending documents across the internet.
