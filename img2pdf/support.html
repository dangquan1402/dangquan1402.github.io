Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert photos to a PDF?

Tap "Select Photos" on the Home tab to choose pictures from your photo library in the sequence you want them. You can also tap the camera icon to scan paper documents or import PDFs from the Files app. Adjust any cropping or filters, choose your export preset, and tap "Share" to export your PDF.

How do I rearrange or reorder pages?

On the document preview screen, press and hold any page thumbnail, then drag it to your desired position. You can also tap the sort button to reverse the page order or sort pages chronologically by photo capture time.

Does img2pdf add a watermark?

No. By default, img2pdf never adds any watermark to your documents. If you want a custom text watermark (for example, "CONFIDENTIAL" or "COPY"), you can enable the optional watermark toggle in export settings and configure the text, position, and opacity.

Do my photos or documents get uploaded to a server?

No. img2pdf operates 100% offline on your iPhone. Document processing, edge detection, OCR text recognition, signing, and file compression are executed inside the app sandbox on your device. Nothing is ever sent to a server.

What page dimensions and layouts are supported?

img2pdf supports US Letter (8.5 x 11 inches), A4 (210 x 297 mm), US Legal, and original image aspect ratios. You can also place 1, 2, or 4 images on each page in a clean grid layout.

Can I edit or clean up images before making a PDF?

Yes. Tap any image thumbnail to crop with four-corner perspective correction, rotate pages, adjust brightness and contrast, or apply automatic document filters that remove shadows from paper scans.

How do I make my PDF searchable?

img2pdf automatically performs on-device OCR on scanned pages and images. Recognised text is embedded directly into the PDF as an invisible layer, allowing you or recipients to search for words and copy text.

How do I delete saved data from the app?

Go to the Recents screen, swipe left on any document to delete it, or tap the menu icon and choose "Clear All History." Deleting the img2pdf app from your iPhone also deletes all sandbox data.
