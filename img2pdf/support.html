Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert photos to PDF?

Tap "Select Photos" on the Home tab, choose your images, adjust settings if needed, then tap "Export PDF" to generate your document.

Can I reorder pages?

Yes. On the Home tab, drag and drop images to rearrange them. You can also reorganize pages after generating a PDF using the Organize button in the preview.

How do I add a watermark?

In the Export settings, toggle on "Show Watermark," type your text, choose a position, and adjust the opacity.

Can I rename a saved PDF?

Yes. Go to the History tab, long-press on any PDF, and select "Rename" from the menu.

Does the app upload my photos?

No. All processing happens on your device. The app works completely offline and never sends your data anywhere.

What page sizes are supported?

US Letter (8.5" x 11"), A4 (210 x 297mm), and Original (matches your image dimensions).

Can I edit images before creating a PDF?

Yes. Tap any image to open the editor where you can crop, rotate, adjust brightness and contrast, or convert to grayscale.

How do I delete the app's data?

Go to History and tap the menu icon to "Clear All History." Deleting the app also removes all stored data.
