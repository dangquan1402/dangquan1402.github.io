Privacy Policy

img2pdf - Photo to PDF Maker · Last updated: April 19, 2026

img2pdf does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All processing happens entirely on your device.

Data Collection

img2pdf does not collect any data. We have no servers, no analytics, no tracking, and no advertising. The app works completely offline.

Photo Library Access

img2pdf requests access to your Photo Library solely to let you select images for PDF conversion. Your photos are:

  • Processed entirely on your device
  • Never uploaded to any server
  • Never shared with any third party
  • Stored locally on your device only when you save a PDF

On-Device Storage

The app stores the following data locally on your device:

  • Images you import for PDF creation (in the app's sandbox)
  • Generated PDF files (in the app's sandbox)
  • Your export preferences and settings (via UserDefaults)

This data is stored only on your device and is removed when you delete the app.

No Internet Connection

img2pdf does not require or use an internet connection. No network requests are made. The app functions entirely offline.

No Third-Party Services

img2pdf does not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics tools, crash reporters, or advertising frameworks.

Children's Privacy

img2pdf does not collect any data from any user, including children. The app is safe for all ages.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, the changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
