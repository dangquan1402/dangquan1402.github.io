What is LinkPocket?

LinkPocket is an iOS bookmark manager designed to help you save links quickly, organize them into custom folders with personalized icons and colors, and stay on top of your reading list with clear read and unread markers.

How to Save a Link

  • iOS Share Sheet: In Safari or any app, tap the Share button and select LinkPocket. LinkPocket generates a link preview and suggests a destination folder so you can save without leaving your current app.
  • Paste from Clipboard: Copy any link in Safari or another app. Open LinkPocket and tap the clipboard banner or the "+" button. On iOS 18+, you can also trigger the "Save copied link" Control Center widget.
  • Browser Extension: Use the LinkPocket browser extension in Chrome, Edge, or Brave to sync bookmarks between your desktop browser and your LinkPocket account.

How to Delete Your Account

You can delete your account and all saved data at any time from within the app:

  1. Open LinkPocket and tap the Account (Settings) icon.
  2. Scroll to the account section and select Delete Account.
  3. Confirm your choice. Your account, bookmarks, and folders will be permanently deleted from our servers.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do category suggestions work?

When you save a link, LinkPocket analyzes the link's title, description, and domain to suggest an appropriate folder using AI categorization powered by TypeSafe. You can confirm the suggested folder, choose another one, or create a new folder on the spot.

How do unread indicators work?

Every newly saved link begins in the unread state with an unread dot. When you tap to open a bookmark, it is marked as read. You can also manually toggle read status at any time or check the Inbox to see all unread links across all folders.

Can I customize my folders?

Yes. Each folder in LinkPocket can be customized with its own icon glyph and accent color swatch, making it easy to identify folders at a glance.

Can I export my bookmarks?

Yes. Open Settings to export your complete bookmark collection as a JSON archive or as a standard Netscape HTML bookmark file compatible with Safari, Chrome, and other desktop browsers.
