Privacy Policy

Smart PDF Compressor · Last updated: April 20, 2026

Smart PDF Compressor does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All PDF processing happens entirely on your device.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. Smart PDF Compressor operates entirely offline and does not require an internet connection to function.

PDF Files

All PDF files you compress are processed locally on your device. Your documents are never uploaded to any server. Compressed files are stored in the app's local storage on your device and are only accessible by you.

No Analytics or Tracking

Smart PDF Compressor does not use any analytics services, tracking pixels, or advertising SDKs. We do not track your usage patterns or behavior.

No Third-Party Services

The app does not integrate with any third-party services. All compression is performed using Apple's built-in PDFKit and image processing frameworks.

Data Storage

Compressed PDF files are stored locally in the app's Documents directory on your device. You can delete all stored files at any time through the app's Settings. Uninstalling the app will remove all stored data.

Children's Privacy

Smart PDF Compressor does not collect any data from any users, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
