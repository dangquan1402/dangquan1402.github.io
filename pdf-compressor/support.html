Frequently Asked Questions

How do I compress a PDF?

Tap the + button on the Home screen, select a PDF file from your device, choose your compression settings, and tap "Compress PDF." The compressed file will be saved automatically.

What compression levels are available?

Three presets: Light (best quality, larger file), Standard (balanced), and Maximum (smallest file). You can also customize image quality, resolution, and grayscale conversion in the advanced settings.

Will compression reduce my PDF quality?

Compression works by reducing image resolution and quality within the PDF. Text-heavy documents may see minimal size reduction. For scanned documents and image-heavy PDFs, you can achieve 80-95% file size reduction.

What happens if compression doesn't reduce the file size?

If compression produces a file that is not significantly smaller (less than 2% reduction), the app automatically keeps your original file and labels it "Original kept" in the history.

Can I compress multiple PDFs at once?

Yes. When selecting files, choose multiple PDFs and the app will compress them in batch with a progress indicator for each file.

How do I share a compressed PDF?

After compression, tap "Share Compressed PDF" on the results screen. You can also tap the share icon next to any file in your history list.

Can I preview compressed PDFs?

Yes. Tap any file in your history to open a full-screen preview. You can also compare the original and compressed versions from the compression results screen.

Can I rename compressed files?

Yes. Swipe right on any file in the history list and tap "Rename," or long-press and select "Rename" from the context menu.

Does the app upload my PDFs to the internet?

No. All processing happens entirely on your device. Your documents never leave your iPhone or iPad. The app works completely offline.

How do I delete compressed files?

Swipe left on any file to delete it, or use Edit mode to select and delete multiple files. You can also clear all history from Settings.

Does the app work on iPad?

Yes. Smart PDF Compressor is a universal app that runs natively on both iPhone and iPad.
