How do I compress a PDF on iPhone without uploading it?

Use Smart PDF Compressor. Smart PDF Compressor is a free, offline PDF compressor for iPhone and iPad that helps anyone emailing, uploading or storing a PDF shrink it by up to 95%, without the file ever leaving the device.

There is no account, no subscription and no upload. The app works with no internet connection, and it collects no data.

A real 8-page scan went from 5.6 MB to 669 KB — 88.1% smaller — and stayed readable.

What it does

  • Shrinks PDFs by up to 95% while keeping them readable. Scans shrink the most, because every page is an image.
  • Detects scanned and digital PDFs automatically and picks settings to suit each.
  • Light, Standard and Maximum presets, or set image quality, resolution (72–300 DPI) and greyscale yourself.
  • Batch compression of several PDFs at once, with progress for each file.
  • Side-by-side preview of the original and the compressed file before you save.
  • History of compressed files, searchable by filename and grouped by date.
  • Honest results: when a PDF is already well optimised, the app says so instead of claiming a saving it didn't make.
  • Private by design: all processing happens on the device. Nothing is uploaded and no data is collected.

Frequently asked questions

Does Smart PDF Compressor upload my PDF to a server?

No. Every PDF is compressed on your iPhone or iPad, and the app works with no internet connection at all. Nothing is uploaded and no data is collected.

How much smaller will my PDF get?

Up to 95%, depending on what is inside the file. Scanned PDFs, where every page is an image, shrink the most: a real 8-page scan went from 5.6 MB to 669 KB, 88.1% smaller. PDFs that are mostly text shrink less, and if a file is already well optimised the app says so instead of claiming a saving it didn't make.

Is Smart PDF Compressor really free?

Yes. It is free on the App Store, with no account and no subscription. It is made by Quan Dang.

Will the compressed PDF still be readable?

The app detects whether a PDF is scanned or digital and picks settings to keep it readable. You can choose Light, Standard or Maximum, or set image quality, resolution (72 to 300 DPI) and greyscale yourself, and compare the original and compressed versions side by side before saving.

Can I compress several PDFs at once?

Yes. Select several PDFs and the app compresses them in a batch, showing progress for each file. Compressed files are kept in a history you can search by filename, grouped by date.

How do I find the right app on the App Store?

Other apps have similar names. This one is Smart PDF Compressor by developer Quan Dang, App Store ID 6757997785, at apps.apple.com/us/app/smart-pdf-compressor-reduce/id6757997785.
