StampName

A Mac app that renames invoices and receipts from what is printed on them. It runs entirely on your Mac, with no account and no uploads.

StampName by Quan Dang · Mac · coming soon to the Mac App Store

What it does

  • Renames from the document: Reads the text printed on PDF and image invoices and receipts and suggests a name for each file, which you review before renaming.
  • Never overwrites: Files are renamed in place and an existing file is never overwritten.
  • Undo: Every rename is recorded locally so it can be undone, and undo is always free.
  • 100% on-device: No server, no account, no analytics. The app cannot connect to the internet, and your document text never leaves your Mac.
  • Pricing: 30 free renames, then StampName Pro as a yearly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase through the App Store.
