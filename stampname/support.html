Frequently Asked Questions

Are my documents uploaded anywhere?

No. StampName runs entirely on your Mac and cannot connect to the internet. See our Privacy Policy.

Can I undo a rename?

Yes. StampName keeps a local journal of your renames so you can undo them. Undo is always free.

How do I restore my StampName Pro purchase?

Use Restore Purchases in StampName while signed in to the App Store with the Apple ID used for the purchase. Restoring is free.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. See Apple’s guide to cancelling a subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid renewal.
