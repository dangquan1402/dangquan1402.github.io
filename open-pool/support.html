What is Open Pool?

Open Pool helps you prepare for the Technician, General, and Amateur Extra exams with practice exams in the real format, topic drills, flashcards, a missed-questions list, saved questions, and the official NCVEC figures. Questions are the public-domain question pools published by the NCVEC Question Pool Committee. Open Pool is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the FCC, NCVEC, ARRL, or any VEC.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which question pools are included?

Technician 2026-2030, General 2023-2027, and Amateur Extra 2024-2028. Each pool is used for exams through June 30 of its final year.

What is free, and what does the purchase unlock?

Open Pool is free to download, and Technician is free with everything included. A one-time in-app purchase (All Access) unlocks General and Amateur Extra: both pools in topic drills, flashcards, and the missed and saved lists, plus practice exams. Before you buy, the first topic of General and of Extra is free to try in topic drills and flashcards. There is no subscription, no ads, and no account. The price is shown in the app before you buy and is set by Apple for each country.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases, or tap Restore Purchases on the unlock screen. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy. Restoring is free and does not charge you again.

Does the purchase work with Family Sharing?

Yes. Members of your Family Sharing group can unlock General and Amateur Extra on their own iPhone without buying again. If it does not show as unlocked, they can tap Restore Purchases.

How do I turn study reminders on or off?

Open Settings in the app and use the Study reminders switch. When reminders are on, you can pick the time of the daily reminder. Turning them off removes every scheduled reminder. You get at most one notification a day and none from 9:30 PM to 8:30 AM. If notifications are turned off for Open Pool in iOS Settings, the app cannot show reminders; see Apple's guide to changing notification settings on iPhone.

How do I reset my progress?

Open Settings in the app and tap the reset button for the license you want to start over, such as Reset Technician progress. This clears your answers, missed and saved questions, and practice exam history for that license only. Your streak and study time stay. A reset cannot be undone.

How are practice exams built?

Each practice exam takes one question from every group in the pool, like the real exam: 35 questions with 26 needed to pass for Technician and General, and 50 questions with 37 needed to pass for Amateur Extra.

How does the missed-questions list work?

Any question you answer incorrectly is added to the Missed list for that exam. It stays there until you answer it correctly.

Where is my progress stored?

Only on your iPhone. There is no account and nothing is sent to the developer. Deleting the app deletes your progress.
