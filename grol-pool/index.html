What it does

  • Practice exams in the real shape: Element 1 exams of 24 questions (18 to pass), Element 3 exams of 100 questions (75 to pass), and Element 8 exams of 50 questions (38 to pass).
  • Study by section: Work through each section of the pool and see your hit rate per section.
  • Smart Study: Spaced repetition brings back the questions you are due to review.
  • Flashcards: Review questions and answers quickly.
  • Missed and saved questions: Revisit the questions you got wrong and the ones you saved.

  • Streaks: Keep a day streak going and track how many questions you answer toward a daily goal you set.
  • Official figures with zoom: Elements 3 and 8 include the figures from the FCC pools; tap a figure to zoom in.
  • Settings: Shuffle answer choices, auto-advance on a right answer, haptic feedback, a daily goal, a practice exam timer, your target license track, and progress reset per element.

  • Private: No account. Your progress stays on your iPhone.

Frequently asked questions

Which exams does GROL Pool cover?

Three FCC commercial radio operator written exams: Element 1 (basic radio law and operating practice: 145-question pool, 24-question exam, 18 to pass), Element 3 (general radiotelephone: 600-question pool, 100-question exam, 75 to pass, with the official figures), and Element 8 (ship radar techniques: 300-question pool, 50-question exam, 38 to pass, with the official figures).

Which question pools are included?

The public-domain FCC question pools: Element 1 as revised in 2017, and Element 3 and Element 8 as approved on June 25, 2009.

What is free?

GROL Pool is free to download and Element 1 is fully free. A single one-time in-app purchase unlocks Element 3 and Element 8; until then, each locked element offers 5 free preview questions per subelement. There is no subscription, no trial, and no ads.

Is GROL Pool affiliated with the FCC?

No. GROL Pool is an independent study app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the FCC or any COLEM.
