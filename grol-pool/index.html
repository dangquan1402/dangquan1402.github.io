GROL Pool

Study for the FCC General Radiotelephone Operator License (GROL) written exams on your iPhone: Element 1 and Element 3, from the official public-domain question pools.

GROL Pool by Quan Dang · iPhone · coming soon to the App Store

What it does

  • Practice exams in the real shape: Element 1 exams of 24 questions (18 to pass) and Element 3 exams of 100 questions (75 to pass).
  • Study by section: Work through each section of the pool and see your hit rate per section.
  • Flashcards: Review questions and answers quickly.
  • Missed and saved questions: Revisit the questions you got wrong and the ones you saved.
  • Official figures: Element 3 includes the figures from the FCC pool.
  • Private: No account. Your progress stays on your iPhone.

Frequently asked questions

Which exams does GROL Pool cover?

The FCC GROL written exams Element 1 (basic radio law and operating practice: 144-question pool, 24-question exam, 18 to pass) and Element 3 (general radiotelephone: 600-question pool, 100-question exam, 75 to pass, with the official figures).

Which question pools are included?

The public-domain FCC question pools for Element 1 and Element 3 approved on June 25, 2009.

What is free?

GROL Pool is free to download and Element 1 is fully free. A single one-time in-app purchase unlocks Element 3. There is no subscription, no trial, and no ads.

Is GROL Pool affiliated with the FCC?

No. GROL Pool is an independent study app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the FCC or any COLEM.
