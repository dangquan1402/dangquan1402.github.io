Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my purchase?

Sign in to the App Store with the same Apple Account you used to unlock Elements 3 and 8, then tap Restore Purchases in GROL Pool (in Settings). Elements 3 and 8 unlock again at no extra charge.

What do the elements cover, and what is free?

Element 1 covers basic radio law and operating practice: a 145-question pool, with exams of 24 questions and 18 correct to pass. Element 3 covers general radiotelephone: a 600-question pool, with exams of 100 questions and 75 correct to pass, including the official figures. Element 8 covers ship radar techniques: a 300-question pool, with exams of 50 questions and 38 correct to pass, including the official figures.

GROL Pool is free to download and Element 1 is fully free. A single one-time in-app purchase unlocks both Element 3 and Element 8. Before you buy, each locked element offers 5 free preview questions per subelement. There is no subscription, no trial, and no ads.

Which question pools are included?

The public-domain FCC question pools: Element 1 as revised in 2017, and Element 3 and Element 8 as approved on June 25, 2009.

Where is my progress stored, and how do I reset it?

Your answers, exam history, Smart Study progress, saved and missed questions, streak, and settings are stored only on your iPhone and are never sent to the developer. To reset progress, use Reset Data in Settings, for one element or for everything. Deleting GROL Pool from your iPhone also deletes all of its data. If you reinstall it, use Restore Purchases to unlock Elements 3 and 8 again.
