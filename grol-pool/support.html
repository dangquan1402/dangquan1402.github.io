Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my purchase?

Sign in to the App Store with the same Apple Account you used to buy Element 3, then tap Restore Purchases in GROL Pool. Element 3 unlocks again at no extra charge.

What do Element 1 and Element 3 cover, and what is free?

Element 1 covers basic radio law and operating practice: a 144-question pool, with exams of 24 questions and 18 correct to pass. Element 3 covers general radiotelephone: a 600-question pool, with exams of 100 questions and 75 correct to pass, including the official figures. Element 1 is fully free. Element 3 is unlocked by a single one-time in-app purchase; there is no subscription and no ads.

Which question pools are included?

The public-domain FCC question pools for Element 1 and Element 3 approved on June 25, 2009.

Where is my progress stored, and how do I reset it?

Your answers, scores, saved and missed questions, and chosen element are stored only on your iPhone and are never sent to the developer. To reset all progress, delete GROL Pool from your iPhone; this deletes all of its data. If you reinstall it, use Restore Purchases to unlock Element 3 again.
