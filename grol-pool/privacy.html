Privacy Policy

GROL Pool · Effective date: October 5, 2026

GROL Pool does not collect any personal data. There is no account, and your study progress never leaves your iPhone.

No Account

GROL Pool does not ask you to sign up or sign in. You can use every part of the app without providing a name, email address, or any other personal information.

What Is Stored on Your Device

To track your study progress, GROL Pool stores the following only on your iPhone:

  • Your answers, exam scores, and exam history.
  • Your Smart Study (spaced repetition) progress and study streak.
  • Your saved questions and missed questions.
  • The exam element you have chosen to study and your app settings.

This data is never sent to the developer or to any server.

No Data Collection, Analytics, Advertising, or Tracking

The developer collects no data from GROL Pool. The app includes no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs, and does not track you across other apps or websites. GROL Pool does not send notifications.

Purchases

The optional in-app purchase that unlocks Elements 3 and 8 is processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive your payment details.

Information Apple May Share

If you have chosen in your iOS settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may provide the developer with aggregated App Store Connect usage statistics and crash reports. This information is collected and processed by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy; it does not identify you to the developer. You can turn this sharing off at any time in your iOS settings.

Deleting Your Data

Deleting GROL Pool from your iPhone deletes all of its data, including your study progress.

Children's Privacy

GROL Pool is not directed toward children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

Any updates to this privacy policy will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
