Privacy Policy

Pasture: Daily Bible Path · Effective date: October 8, 2026

Pasture does not collect any personal data. There is no account, and your progress never leaves your iPhone.

No Account

Pasture does not ask you to sign up or sign in. You can use every part of the app without providing an email address or any other personal information.

What Is Stored on Your Device

To remember your progress, Pasture stores the following only on your iPhone:

  • The answers you chose during setup: your goal, your experience level, your daily reading time, and the path you are on.
  • Your completed lessons and quiz scores.
  • Your day streak and any streak freezes.
  • Your lamb companion's name and growth.
  • The book and chapter you last opened in the Bible reader.
  • Whether you have an active Pasture Premium subscription.

This data is never sent to the developer or to any server. The app has no server of its own, and the Bible text and lessons are built into the app, so Pasture works without an internet connection.

No Data Collection, Analytics, Advertising, or Tracking

The developer collects no data from Pasture. The app includes no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs, shows no ads, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Pasture can send an optional daily reminder. It is off until you turn it on, it is scheduled on your device by iOS, and no information leaves your device to send it. You can turn it off any time in the app or in iOS Settings.

Purchases

Pasture Premium subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store using StoreKit. The developer does not receive your name, Apple ID, or payment details. The app only checks with Apple whether your subscription is active.

Information Apple May Share

If you have chosen in your iOS settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may provide the developer with aggregated App Store Connect usage statistics and crash reports. This information is collected and processed by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy; it does not identify you to the developer. You can turn this sharing off at any time in your iOS settings.

Deleting Your Data

Deleting Pasture from your iPhone deletes all of its data, including your progress, streak, and companion. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription; see Support for how to cancel.

Children's Privacy

Pasture is not directed toward children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, none is collected from children.

Changes to This Policy

Any updates to this privacy policy will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
