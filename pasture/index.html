Pasture: Daily Bible Path

Short daily Bible lessons on your iPhone, one step at a time, with a lamb companion to keep you company and the full World English Bible to read offline.

Pasture by Quan Dang · iPhone · coming soon to the App Store

What it does

  • Daily lessons: Each day is a short Scripture passage, a brief explanation, a prayer prompt, and a quick quiz on what you read.
  • Three guided paths: First Steps: 30 Days with God (free), Peace & Prayer: 14 Days, and Meet Jesus: Mark in 30 Days.
  • A lamb companion: Name your lamb during setup and watch it grow as you finish lessons.
  • Day streak: Keep a streak going by finishing a lesson each day.
  • The whole Bible, offline: All 66 books of the World English Bible are built into the app.
  • Pricing: Free download with the First Steps path and the Bible reader free. Pasture Premium is an auto-renewing subscription ($4.99 a month or $29.99 a year in the US, with a 7-day free trial for eligible new subscribers) that unlocks the Premium paths and streak freezes. The first 3 lessons of each Premium path are free. No ads.
  • Private: No account, no analytics, no tracking. Your progress stays on your iPhone.

Frequently asked questions

What is a daily lesson in Pasture?

Each lesson is one day on a path: a short Scripture passage, a brief explanation, a prayer prompt, and a quick quiz on what you read. Finishing a lesson keeps your day streak going and helps your lamb companion grow.

Which paths are included, and what is free?

Pasture has three paths. First Steps: 30 Days with God is free. Peace & Prayer: 14 Days and Meet Jesus: Mark in 30 Days are part of Pasture Premium, and the first 3 lessons of each are free. The full Bible reader is free.

How much is Pasture Premium?

Pasture Premium is an auto-renewing subscription: $4.99 a month or $29.99 a year in the US. Prices may vary by country and are shown in the app before you subscribe. Each plan starts with a 7-day free trial for eligible new subscribers. Premium unlocks the Premium paths and streak freezes.

Which Bible translation does Pasture use?

The World English Bible, a public-domain translation, unchanged from eBible.org. All 66 books are built into the app, so you can read them without an internet connection.

Scripture quotations are from the World English Bible (public domain), unchanged from eBible.org. Lessons are study aids, not doctrine or professional advice.
