What is Pasture?

Pasture offers short daily Bible lessons on guided paths. Each lesson is a short Scripture passage, a brief explanation, a prayer prompt, and a quick quiz. A lamb companion you name grows as you finish lessons, and a day streak tracks your rhythm. The full World English Bible is built into the app for reading offline. There is no account, no ads, and no tracking.

There are three paths: First Steps: 30 Days with God (free), Peace & Prayer: 14 Days, and Meet Jesus: Mark in 30 Days. The last two are part of Pasture Premium, and their first 3 lessons are free.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases, or tap Restore Purchases at the bottom of the Premium screen. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe. Restoring is free and does not charge you again.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Pasture Premium is managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Pasture, and tap Cancel Subscription. See Apple's guide to cancelling a subscription. To avoid being charged, cancel at least 24 hours before your free trial or current period ends; you keep Premium until the end of the period you have paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

How much does Pasture Premium cost?

$4.99 a month or $29.99 a year in the US, each with a 7-day free trial for eligible new subscribers. Prices may vary by country and are shown in the app before you subscribe. Premium unlocks the Peace & Prayer and Meet Jesus paths and streak freezes. See the Terms of Use for the full subscription terms.

Where is my progress stored?

Only on your iPhone. There is no account and nothing is sent to the developer or to any server. Your lessons, streak, companion, and settings are not synced to other devices, and deleting the app deletes them. See the Privacy Policy.

Which Bible translation does Pasture use?

The World English Bible, a public-domain modern English translation, unchanged from eBible.org. All 66 books are included and work without an internet connection.

I found an error in a lesson or a verse. How do I report it?

Thank you for telling us. Email ohioa4@gmail.com with the path name, the day number of the lesson, and what looks wrong (for a quiz question, include the question and the answer you expected). For Scripture text, include the book, chapter, and verse.

Is Pasture a substitute for counseling or medical care?

No. Lessons are study aids. If worry, anxiety, or low mood is heavy or does not lift, please talk with a doctor or counselor. If you are in crisis or may be in danger, contact your local emergency services right away.

How do I contact support?

If you experience any issues or have feedback, send an email to ohioa4@gmail.com.
