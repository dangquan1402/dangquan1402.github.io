Privacy-First Architecture

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 1, 2026

ProofPop ("we," "our," or "the App") is developed to help creators, merchants, and founders transform customer reviews and direct messages into high-converting visual stickers and ad assets. We respect your privacy and believe your photos and business data should remain entirely in your control.

1. 100% On-Device Processing

When you import screenshots or photos into ProofPop, all text recognition (OCR), visual detection, avatar cropping, smart highlights, and redactions are executed locally on your device using Apple's native Vision framework. Your photos, screenshots, and customer messages are never uploaded to any remote server or third-party cloud service.

2. No Account Required

ProofPop requires no sign-up, registration, or login. We do not collect, request, or store your name, email address, password, or any profile information.

3. Zero Third-Party Analytics or Tracking

We do not include third-party tracking SDKs, behavioral analytics platforms, or advertising networks. ProofPop does not track your app usage, monitor your activity across other apps or websites, or share identifiers with third parties.

4. Photo Library Access

ProofPop requests access to your Photos library solely to allow you to select screenshots for editing and to save exported sticker images or videos back to your library. We only access photos you explicitly select or export.

5. In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

All in-app purchases and subscriptions (such as ProofPop Pro) are processed securely and directly by Apple via StoreKit. We do not have access to your credit card information, bank details, or billing address. Transactions are governed by Apple's Media Services Terms and Conditions.
