Privacy Policy

CreatorRoll · Effective date: October 25, 2026

CreatorRoll is built so your creative video work stays entirely yours. Everything runs on your iPhone. There is no account, no analytics, no ads, and nothing is uploaded to any server.

Data Not Collected

We collect no data. CreatorRoll has no account registration, no sign-in, no telemetry or analytics SDKs, and no servers of its own. Under Apple's App Store privacy labels, CreatorRoll is classified as “Data Not Collected”.

Photos Library Access

CreatorRoll requests permission to access your Apple Photos library solely on your device. This permission is used exclusively to:

  • Read video durations, recording dates, and metadata to group shoot takes together on your iPhone.
  • Play back and preview video clips locally within the app.
  • Identify potential false starts and suggest keeper takes directly on your device.
  • Request deletion of unwanted outtakes through Apple Photos when you choose to discard them.

Your photos and videos are processed entirely on your device. They are never uploaded to any remote server, never analyzed in the cloud, and never shared with the developer or any third party.

Deletions via Apple Photos

CreatorRoll does not permanently bypass your device's photo protections. When you choose to delete outtakes or false starts:

  • Deletions are requested through Apple's Photos framework, which prompts you with a standard system confirmation dialog.
  • Deleted videos are moved to the system Recently Deleted album in Apple Photos, where they remain recoverable for 30 days before permanent deletion.
  • You can review or restore deleted items at any time during those 30 days in the Apple Photos app. See Apple's guide to deleting and recovering photos and videos on iPhone.

What Stays on Your iPhone

All app-specific data, including your shoot protection flags, keeper selections, and application preferences, is stored locally in your iPhone's private application sandbox. If you delete CreatorRoll from your device, these local preferences are removed. Standard device backups (such as backups made to your computer or through your Apple ID) may include app data under Apple's terms; the developer has no access to those backups.

Subscriptions and Purchases

CreatorRoll Pro subscriptions are processed directly by Apple through the App Store using StoreKit 2. The developer never receives or stores your payment card numbers, billing details, or Apple ID account information. CreatorRoll checks your subscription status locally on your device with Apple.

Diagnostic Information Apple May Share

If you have enabled sharing of diagnostic and usage information with app developers in your iOS settings, Apple may provide the developer with aggregated crash logs and system metrics. This information is collected and processed under Apple's Privacy Policy; it does not contain video footage, filenames, or personal identifiers.

Children's Privacy

CreatorRoll does not collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

Any updates to this privacy policy will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
