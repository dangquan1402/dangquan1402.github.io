What is Gracebloom?

Gracebloom lets you place daily Bible verses over your own photos on your Home Screen, Lock Screen, and StandBy. It includes a private prayer journal with iOS Data Protection, audio verse readings with calming music beds, and the complete text of the World English Bible (English) and Reina-Valera 1909 (Spanish) available completely offline. There is no user account, no advertising, and no tracking.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add Gracebloom widgets to my Home Screen, Lock Screen, or StandBy?

Gracebloom supports widgets across your iPhone surfaces. For step-by-step guidance on widget management in iOS, see Apple's guide to adding and editing widgets on iPhone.

  • Home Screen: Touch and hold an empty area on your Home Screen until the app icons jiggle. Tap Edit in the top-left corner, tap Add Widget, search for Gracebloom, choose your preferred size, and tap Add Widget.
  • Lock Screen: Touch and hold your Lock Screen, tap Customize, choose Lock Screen, and tap the widget area below or above the clock to add Gracebloom.
  • StandBy: While your iPhone is charging on its side in landscape orientation, swipe to the widgets screen, touch and hold any widget, tap +, and select Gracebloom.
How do I restore my purchase?

Open Gracebloom Settings or tap the Restore link on the Gracebloom Premium screen. Make sure you are signed in to the App Store with the same Apple ID you used for the original purchase. Restoring is free and will not charge your account again.

Is Spanish supported?

Yes. Gracebloom includes the complete Spanish Reina-Valera 1909 (RV1909) public-domain Bible translation alongside the English World English Bible (WEB). You can switch between English and Spanish scripture inside the app's settings. The app interface also supports Spanish based on your iOS language settings.

How does audio playback work?

Gracebloom includes spoken audio for daily verses and devotional reflections. When recorded voice clips are available, they play automatically. If a recording is not present on your device, the app reads the scripture using Apple's installed system voices via AVSpeechSynthesizer. Gracebloom Premium allows you to mix in gentle background music beds (such as morning piano, hymns, or ambient tracks) and nature sounds (like soft rain or fire), adjust playback speed, and set a sleep timer.

What is free, and what does Gracebloom Premium include?

Gracebloom is free to download. The daily verse of the day, the offline Bible reader (both English and Spanish), and core spoken audio are completely free. Gracebloom Premium unlocks custom widget photo backgrounds, all background music beds and ambient soundscapes, bedtime and topical listening playlists, and unlimited prayer journal themes. Premium is offered as a weekly subscription ($4.99/week), a yearly subscription ($34.99/year with a 7-day free trial for eligible new subscribers), or a Lifetime one-time unlock ($59.99). See the Terms of Use for details.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name (Apple ID) at the top, select Subscriptions, tap Gracebloom, and choose Cancel Subscription. To avoid being charged, cancel at least 24 hours before your free trial or current billing period ends. See Apple's guide to cancelling a subscription.

Where are my photos and prayer entries stored?

All data stays on your device. Resized copies of photos you choose for widgets are stored in an on-device App Group shared only between Gracebloom and its widget. Prayer journal entries are stored locally with iOS Data Protection. There are no servers, no accounts, and nothing is uploaded. See our Privacy Policy.
