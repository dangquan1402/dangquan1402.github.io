Privacy Policy

Gracebloom: Bible Verse Widget · Effective date: October 13, 2026

Gracebloom is built so that your faith life stays yours. There is no account, no analytics, no ads, and nothing leaves your iPhone.

What We Collect

Nothing. Gracebloom has no account, no sign-in, no analytics frameworks, no advertising SDKs, and no servers of its own.

What Stays on Your iPhone

Gracebloom stores all app data locally on your device:

  • Photos you pick for widgets: A resized copy is saved in the app's private on-device storage (in an App Group shared with the widget) so your widget can display it. Your photos are never uploaded to any server.
  • Prayer journal entries: Stored locally in a private file on your device with iOS Data Protection.
  • Your streak, chosen themes, widget style, and reminder times: Stored locally on your device.

If you delete Gracebloom from your iPhone, this data is removed. Standard device backups (such as backups made to your computer or through your Apple ID) may include this data under Apple's terms. The developer cannot access those backups.

Notifications

Daily verse reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No push server is involved.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store using StoreKit. The developer does not receive your name, email address, or payment details. Gracebloom only checks locally with Apple on your device whether you have an active purchase.

Sharing

When you share a verse image or video (using Share Studio), the file is generated on your device and handed to the destination or application you select. What happens next depends on the application you share with.

Information Apple May Share

If you have enabled sharing of diagnostic and usage information with app developers in your iOS settings, Apple may provide the developer with aggregated crash reports and usage statistics. This information is collected and processed by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy; it does not identify you personally.

Children's Privacy

Gracebloom is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Scripture

Scripture verses are from the World English Bible (English) and Reina-Valera 1909 (Spanish), both of which are in the public domain.

Changes to This Policy

Any updates to this privacy policy will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
