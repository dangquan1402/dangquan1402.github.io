Frequently asked questions

What is Gracebloom?

Gracebloom is an iPhone app that brings Scripture into your daily rhythm through customizable widgets on your Home Screen, Lock Screen, and StandBy. You can pair verses with your personal photos, keep a private prayer journal, and listen to spoken scripture and audio reflections.

Which Bible translations are included?

Gracebloom includes the World English Bible (WEB) for English and the Reina-Valera 1909 (RV1909) for Spanish. Both translations are in the public domain and stored entirely on your device so you can read and listen offline.

How does Gracebloom protect my privacy?

Gracebloom requires no account or sign-in, contains no analytics or advertising SDKs, and has no servers. Your photos, prayer journal entries, streaks, and preferences stay on your iPhone and are shared only with the Gracebloom widget via a local App Group.

What is free, and what does Gracebloom Premium include?

Gracebloom is free to download and use with the verse of the day, offline Bible reader, and core audio. Gracebloom Premium unlocks all widget photo customization, all music beds and ambient backgrounds, bedtime and topical audio playlists, and unlimited prayer journal themes. Premium is available as a weekly subscription ($4.99/week), yearly subscription ($34.99/year with a 7-day free trial), or a lifetime one-time purchase ($59.99).

Scripture quotations are from the World English Bible and Reina-Valera 1909 (public domain). Lessons and reflections are study aids, not doctrine or professional advice.
